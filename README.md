# Chenyu Li

I am a PhD student at the [Laboratoire de Linguistique Formelle](https://www.llf.cnrs.fr) ([Université Paris Cité](https://u-pariscite.fr) and [CNRS](https://www.cnrs.fr/fr)).

My research interests include the phonetics–phonology interface, dynamic modelling of speech, sociophonetics, cross-variety study, corpus-based research, and laboratory phonology.

I investigate the temporal patterns of vowel production and the relationships between acoustic and articulatory trajectories, e.g., *f*0 and formants. My work combines generalized additive mixed models (GAMMs), Bayesian regression, functional data analysis (fPCA, Function-on-Function mapping), and machine learning, with theoretical perspectives on speech dynamics, including Articulatory Phonology.

## Research materials

### Diphthong Dynamics under Lexical Tone: Cross-Dialectal Evidence for Category-Specific f0–F1 Coupling

Supplementary documents and example R scripts accompany this study of tone–vowel relations in Mandarin diphthongs.

The materials include:

- Supplementary methods, additional results, and robustness analyses.
- GAMM summaries, diagnostic checks, and model comparisons.
- Core R scripts for GAMMs, functional principal component analysis (fPCA), and function-on-function regression (FoF).
